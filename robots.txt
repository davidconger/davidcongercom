# Deliberately minimal. robots.txt is a public file, so every Disallow line is
# a signpost to the very thing it is meant to hide -- and worse, a disallowed
# URL cannot be crawled, which means a `noindex` on that page is never read and
# the URL can still surface in results on the strength of inbound links alone.
# Anything that should stay out of search is handled in web.config instead,
# with an X-Robots-Tag header on the paths themselves.
