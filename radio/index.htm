Starting in 2005, Dave has been on the weekends at 106.1 KISS-FM Seattle. Until April 2009, Dave was heard Saturdays from 5p-10p and various times on Sundays. Dave hosted the Club KISS Warm-Up with Victor Menegaux Saturdays at 9p. Featuring all new CHR music, New KISS 2 is an HD multicast station of 106.1 KISS-FM. In early 2008, Dave began writing and producing the imaging for NK2. Dave designed and built the NK2 website & coordinated the online presence for the station.

  David started in radio at the University of Puget Sound's KUPS "the Sound" in 2001. Dave worked as On-Air Talent through 2005, Hip-Hop Music Director from 2002-2004, Webmaster from 2003-2005, Assistant Programming Director in 2004, and Programming Director from 2004-2005. Dave was a finalist for the CBI National Production Award for Best Radio DJ in 2004. KUPS was nominated for the CMJ College Radio Awards in 2005 for Most Improved Station while Dave was PD. Dave starting interning at KBKS in 2003.
