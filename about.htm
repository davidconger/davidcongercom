About David Conger Photography

I am a photographer based in Seattle, Washington, photographing hundreds of concerts, festivals, and events each year. I pride myself on delivering rich, high-quality images with extreme speed. Most events covered are represented on social media while the event is still going on by the outlets and artists that I work with, thanks to a well-tuned workflow and the use of new technology.
