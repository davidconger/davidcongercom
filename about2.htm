About David Conger Photography
I am a photographer based in Seattle, Washington, photographing hundreds of concerts, festivals, and events each year. I pride myself rich high quality images provided with extreme speed. Most events covered are represented on social media while the event is still going on by the outlets and artists that I work with due to a well tune workflow and use of new technology.
